### Writing? Why would I need writing?
Writing brings clarity. Even in an age of LLM-powered generative AI, sitting down with pen-to-paper (or fingers-to-keyboard) forces thoughts to be structured into a coherent whole if they are to be understood by anyone other than the one who thought them.
