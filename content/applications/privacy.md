# Privacy Policy
*Last updated: September 11, 2026*

At **Neward & Associates**, we respect your privacy. Our software is designed so that we collect only the minimal information necessary to function, and we do not sell or share your personal information.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store personal information such as:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Your contacts, photos, or files
- Your browsing history or activity across other websites
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Payment information, unless required to complete a transaction through a third-party payment provider

IP addresses may be captured by intermediate infrastructure (such as the Apple or Google app stores), but we do not receive that information directly. Some information may be provided to us in reports provided by those intermediaries, but we do not engage in any deeper analysis than what the report provides, and we do not provide access to those reports to anyone outside of ourselves.

## 2. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or license personal information to anyone.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising or allow third parties to track you across websites or apps for advertising purposes.

## 3. How Our Service Works

Our service is designed to operate without requiring us to collect or retain personal information.

If information must be processed temporarily to provide a feature you request, we process it only for that purpose and do not retain it after the feature has been provided, except where retention is technically necessary or required by law.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Some parts of our service may rely on third-party providers, such as hosting, security, analytics, or payment services. We choose providers that support our privacy commitments and configure our services to minimize the information made available to them.

Where a third party independently collects information through its own service, its privacy policy governs that information.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or similar technologies to track you for advertising or profiling purposes.

We may use strictly necessary technologies that are required for the basic operation, security, or functionality of our service.

## 6. Data Security

Although we do not maintain a database of user personal information, we use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect our systems and the information processed while providing our service.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you discover a means by which our software is used to violate childrens' privacy or collect personal information from them, we absolutely want to know about it, and encourage you to reach out to us.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide appropriate notice through our website or service.

The date at the top of this policy indicates when it was most recently updated.
