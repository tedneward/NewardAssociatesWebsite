Perfect for foursomes playing Spades around a table, particularly on a regular basis.

Features:

- Clean, simple design
- Full game history, allowing review of every round
- Bid nullo for a 100 (or customized) point boost
- Behind even farther? Bid blind nullo and go up 250 (or customizable amounts of) points!
- iCloud sync - start a game on your phone, save it, pick it up again later, even on another device
- Color themes, include both light and dark modes
- Rules of Spades included in the app

Game options:

- Blind nullo can optionally be auto-bid by the losing team (if they're behind far enough)
- Blind nullo can optionally be set to allow a pass of cards, for lower points gained
- Everybody keeps going set? Set a "losing threshold" along with a "winning threshold"!
- Want a faster game? Set the winning threshold to 50 points. Or make a marathon of it at 5,000!
- Taunts! The app helps keep it all competitive by chirping from the sidelines.
- Statistics! Which teams do best? Who makes their blind nullo bids more often?

The app is $0.99, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
