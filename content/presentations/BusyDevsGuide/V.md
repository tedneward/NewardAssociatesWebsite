V is a new language that describes itself as "simple, fast, safe, and compiled." It brings together language ideas from languages like Kotlin and Swift together with "oldies but goodies" like Oberon and C. It has a flexible memory allocation scheme, easy interop with other languages, and a syntax that will be familiar to most mainstream developers, as well as a screaming-fast compiler that can emit native, JavaScript, or WebAssembly images.

In this presentation, we're going to take a look at this emerging language and give it a good kick in the tires.
